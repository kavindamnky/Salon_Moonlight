## 🌟 Overview

MOONLIGHT SALON is a next-generation digital experience crafted for modern beauty brands. It combines luxury design, performance optimization, and user-friendly booking systems to enhance client engagement and streamline salon operations.

This platform is designed to reflect elegance, trust, and premium service while ensuring smooth performance across all devices.

---

## 🚀 Features

> These are the features that elevate Moonlight Salon to a luxury digital standard.

- **Online Booking System:** Clients can book appointments in real-time with service and staff selection.
- **Luxury UI/UX:** White & gold themed interface with smooth animations and hover effects.
- **Responsive Design:** Fully optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
- **WhatsApp Integration:** One-click chat for instant customer support and inquiries.
- **Service Gallery:** High-quality image showcase for treatments, styles, and transformations.
- **Admin-Friendly Structure:** Easy to manage and scale for future updates.
- **Performance Optimized:** Fast loading with modern build tools and best practices.
